What Are Financial Frauds?

Financial frauds involve dishonest activities carried out to illegally obtain money, assets, or sensitive financial information from individuals or organizations. With increasing digital payments and online banking in India, financial fraud cases have significantly increased.

Common Financial Frauds in India

  • Investment & trading scams
  • Fake loan and instant credit app fraud
  • Credit/Debit card fraud
  • Insurance and policy renewal scams
  • SIM swap and identity theft

How Financial Frauds Happen

  • Creating fear or urgency such as account suspension or legal action
  • Impersonating banks, RBI, police, or government departments
  • Fake websites, mobile apps, and social media advertisements
  • Phishing messages collecting personal and banking information
  • Misuse of leaked data from online platforms
Warning: Fraudsters often promise guaranteed returns or instant benefits to trap victims.

Precautions & Safety Measures

  • Never share OTP, PIN, CVV, or passwords with anyone
  • Verify investment schemes with SEBI-registered entities
  • Download apps only from official app stores
  • Enable SMS and email alerts for bank transactions
  • Immediately report fraud to helpline 1930
