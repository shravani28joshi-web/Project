About UPI Scams in India

UPI (Unified Payments Interface) scams are one of the most common cyber crimes reported in India today. Fraudsters exploit trust, urgency, and lack of awareness to trick users into authorizing transactions or sharing confidential details.

Common Types of UPI Scams

  • Collect Request Scam: Victims are asked to "approve" a request to receive money, but approval actually debits money.
  • QR Code Scam: Scammers send QR codes claiming refunds, prizes, or payments. Scanning a QR code can only SEND money.
  • Fake Customer Care Scam: Fraudsters pose as bank or app support executives and trick users into sharing PINs or installing apps.
  • Remote Access Scam: Victims are asked to install apps like AnyDesk or TeamViewer, giving scammers full phone control.
  • Job & Cashback Scams: Fake part-time jobs or cashback offers lure users into making small payments that escalate.

Golden Rule: Never share your UPI PIN, OTP, or click on unknown payment links.

Phishing Attacks Occurring in India

Phishing attacks are fraudulent attempts to steal personal, financial, or login information by impersonating trusted institutions such as banks, RBI, government portals, or courier services.

Common Phishing Methods

  • SMS Phishing (Smishing): Messages claiming KYC expiry, account blocking, or refunds.
  • Email Phishing: Fake emails resembling banks or payment apps asking to verify details.
  • WhatsApp Phishing: Messages with malicious links, fake customer support chats, or prize offers.
  • Website Spoofing: Fake websites that closely resemble official bank or government portals.
  • Social Media Phishing: Fake ads and pages offering giveaways or investment schemes.

How to Identify Phishing

  • Urgent or threatening language
  • Suspicious links or shortened URLs
  • Spelling and grammatical errors
  • Requests for PINs, OTPs, or passwords

If you suspect phishing, do not click links and report immediately.
