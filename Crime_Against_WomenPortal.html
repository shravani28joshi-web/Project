About Crimes Against Women

Crimes against women include acts of violence, harassment, exploitation, and discrimination that threaten the safety, dignity, and rights of women. Addressing these crimes is a priority under Indian law and the Digital Police initiative.

Emergency: Dial 112 for immediate police help | Women Helpline: 181

Common Crimes Against Women

  • Domestic violence
  • Sexual harassment & assault
  • Cyber stalking and online harassment
  • Dowry-related harassment
  • Human trafficking
  • Workplace harassment

Women Safety & Legal Rights

  • Right to file FIR at any police station (Zero FIR)
  • Right to privacy and confidentiality
  • Protection under IPC & IT Act
  • Free legal aid and medical assistance
  • Right to report cyber crimes online

If you face harassment or violence, report it immediately to ensure safety and justice.
