Learn About Cyber Crime

1. Viruses, Worms and Trojans ▼
Malware that damages systems, spreads automatically, or disguises itself as legitimate software.
2. Cyber Phishing ▼
Fraudulent emails or websites used to steal passwords, OTPs, and banking details.
3. Cyber Vishing ▼
Voice-based fraud where attackers impersonate officials or banks.
4. SIM Swap Scam ▼
Fraudsters take control of a victim’s mobile number to access OTPs and bank accounts.
5. Spamming ▼
Sending unsolicited bulk messages containing malicious or fraudulent content.
6. Online Job Frauds ▼
Fake job offers demanding fees or personal information.
7. Cyber Stalking ▼
Repeated online harassment or monitoring through digital platforms.
